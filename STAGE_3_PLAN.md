# Stage 3: Deeper Deckbuilder — Detailed Plan

**Goal**: Full deckbuilder feel with card upgrades, 11 new cards, player annotation depth, and campaign expansion to ~8 floors.

**Starting state**: 3 playable floors, 11 cards (5 starter + 6 reward), 135 tests, no upgrade system, no player-initiated annotations.

**End state**: 8 playable floors, 22+ cards, card upgrade/removal system, player annotation system with flagging, manhattan-2 adjacency, ~215+ tests.

## Complete Stage 3 Card Reference

### New Targeting Cards

#### Argue (alpha: Argument)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: 1 tile (center of 3x3 area)
- **Base effect**: Find all neutral tiles in the 3x3 area and annotate them with their exact owner type ({Neutral}). Annotate the rest as not neutral.
- **Enhanced effect**: Same, plus draw 1 card.
- **Notes**: Unrevealed tiles only.

#### Eavesdrop (alpha: Eavesdropping)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: 1 tile
- **Base effect**: Without revealing the tile, learn whether it's yours (annotate as {Player}) or not yours (annotate as {Rival, Neutral, Noble}). Also get player adjacency info on the tile (how many of the tile's neighbors are player tiles).
- **Enhanced effect**: Learn the tile's exact owner type. Also get full adjacency info for all owner types (player, neutral, rival, noble counts).
- **Notes**: Does not reveal the tile. Does not end your turn regardless of owner.

#### Peek (alpha: Canary)
- **Cost**: 0 spoons
- **Exhaust**: Conditional — only if any nobles were found; otherwise discarded normally
- **Targeting**: 1 tile (center of burst-1-cross area: the tile plus its 4 cardinal neighbors)
- **Base effect**: Check all unrevealed tiles in the burst-1-cross for nobles. Any noble tiles found are annotated as {Noble} and the rest as {Player, Rival, Neutral}. If at least one noble was found, Peek is exhausted. If no nobles found, Peek is discarded normally.
- **Enhanced effect**: 3x3 area instead of burst-1-cross.
- **Notes**: n/a

#### Accept Help (alpha: Horse)
- **Cost**: 3 spoons
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: 1 tile (center of burst-1-cross area)
- **Base effect**: Among all unrevealed tiles in the burst-1-cross, find those of the safest owner type (Player > Neutral > Rival > Noble). Reveal all tiles of that safest type. After playing, all future Accept Help cards cost 0 spoons for the rest of this floor (status effect).
- **Enhanced effect**: Annotate with exact owner type instead of revealing. (All other tiles in the cross get annotated as "anything less safe than whatever was the safest".)
- **Notes**: Safety ranking matches Scurry. If the safest type is not Player, revealing those tiles will end your turn. If a tile is ExtraDirty, it gets cleaned instead of revealed and annotated with its owner type. The 0-cost status effect persists for the floor. A bonus-spoon Accept Help, after a prior Accept Help was played, costs 0 but still grants 1 spoon.
- **Targeting area**: Burst-1-cross = center + N/S/E/W (5 tiles max).

#### Explode (alpha: Emanation)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: 1 tile (any unrevealed tile)
- **Base effect**: Destroy the targeted tile (remove it from the board permanently). Gain 1 Complaint (status effect, gain 1 stack of it, when floor ends you lose 2 copper per stack of Complaint). Add 1 Mollify card to your hand. (Mollify is a 1-cost card which removes one stack of Complaint and exhausts).
- **Enhanced effect**: Destroy the tile without gaining Complaints or adding Mollify to your hand.
- **Notes**: If you destroy the final player tile, you complete the floor. If you destroy the final rival tile, you lose. If you destroy a noble tile, fine.

#### Deliver (alpha: Snip, Snip)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: 1 tile (any unrevealed tile)
- **Base effect**: If the tile is a noble, convert it to neutral, reveal it, and show player adjacency info on it. This does NOT end your turn. If any noble was defused, gain 2 copper. Also removes any surface mines on the tile (Stage 5 mechanic).
- **Enhanced effect**: Additionally get noble adjacency info on the tile, whether or not the tile was actually a noble.
- **Notes**: If the tile isn't a noble, the base effect does nothing (no reveal, no copper). Enhanced gives you noble adjacency info either way, making it useful even on non-noble tiles. The reveal-without-turn-ending is the key mechanic — it safely neutralizes nobles.

#### Brat (alpha: Brat)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: Yes
- **Targeting**: 1 tile (must be a **revealed** tile — unique targeting requirement)
- **Base effect**: Unreveal the tile. The tile keeps any adjacency info and annotations it had. Then exhaust Brat.
- **Enhanced effect**: Same, plus gain 2 copper.
- **Notes**: This is the only card that targets revealed tiles. Unrevealing a tile means the rival loses information about it. More useful on later floors with smarter rival AI. The tile goes back to being unrevealed but retains all annotations from before.

### New Immediate Cards

#### Ramble (alpha: Ramble)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: None (immediate)
- **Base effect**: Add 2 stacks of Distraction to the rival. Each stack adds random noise (~0.75 pips) to every tile the rival's AI is evaluating on their next turn.
- **Enhanced effect**: Add 4 stacks instead of 2.
- **Notes**: Distraction is a status effect. Requires the rival AI's probabilistic evaluation system to have any mechanical effect. In Stage 3 with the simple AI, this card has no gameplay impact — it becomes meaningful in Stage 5 when Conservative/Reasoning AI is implemented. Still worth implementing the Distraction status tracking now.

#### Glaze (alpha: Underwire)
- **Cost**: 0 spoons
- **Exhaust**: Base exhausts; Enhanced does NOT exhaust
- **Targeting**: None (immediate)
- **Base effect**: Gain 1 stack of Excuses for this floor. The next time you would reveal a noble (or trigger a surface mine), the protection is consumed instead and you survive. Then exhaust Glaze.
- **Enhanced effect**: Same protection, but Glaze is discarded normally instead of exhausted (reusable!).
- **Notes**: Multiple Glazes stack — each noble reveal consumes one stack. Excuses is consumed before Grace (if Grace is ever implemented). This is a critical safety card for floors with nobles.

### Deck Manipulation Cards

#### Mask (alpha: Masking)
- **Cost**: 0 spoons
- **Exhaust**: Base exhausts; Enhanced does NOT exhaust
- **Targeting**: Card selection from hand (new targeting mode — player picks another card in their hand)
- **Base effect**: Choose another card in your hand. Play it for free (costs 0 spoons regardless of its normal cost). Then exhaust both Mask and the played card.
- **Enhanced effect**: Only the played card is exhausted. Mask is discarded normally instead.
- **Notes**: The played card's targeting still applies — if you Mask a Spritz, you still need to select a target tile. If the played card has bonus spoon, the bonus still triggers (your spoons go up!). Mask + Caffeinate = gain 2 spoons for free. Mask + Accept Help = free 3-spoon card.

#### Nap (alpha: Nap)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: Yes
- **Targeting**: Card selection from exhaust pile (new targeting mode — player picks from exhaust)
- **Base effect**: Return a card (other than another Nap) from your exhaust pile to your hand. Then exhaust Nap.
- **Enhanced effect**: Same, plus gain spoons equal to the retrieved card's cost.
- **Notes**: If the exhaust pile is empty, Nap does nothing but exhausts. The retrieved card can be played immediately if you have spoons. Nap exhausts itself, so you can't Nap to retrieve Nap.

#### Mollify (generated card, alpha: Evidence)
- **Cost**: 1 spoon
- **Exhaust**: Yes
- **Targeting**: None (immediate)
- **Base effect**: Reduce stacks of Complaints (status effect) by 1.
- **Enhanced effect**: N/A (Mollify cards cannot be enhanced)
- **Notes**: Created by Explode (base version) and by Grace (eventual status effect). Mollify is added directly to your hand, not your deck. It does NOT persist between floors — Mollify cards are removed from your deck at floor end.

### Instruction Variants (Deferred to Stage 4)

#### Recall - Vague (alpha: Vague Instructions)
- **Cost**: 2 spoons
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: None (immediate)
- **Base effect**: Distribute 10 green clue pips (rounded, same as Imperious) across unrevealed tiles. Five player tiles are chosen as targets (vs 2 for Imperious). Weaker signal per tile but broader coverage — roughly 3-6 of the pipped tiles will be yours.
- **Enhanced effect**: More guaranteed draws from the player-tile pool (5 guaranteed vs 3 base).
- **Bag composition**: 5 player targets × 4 copies each = 20. 14 spoilers: neutral/rival × 2 copies, player/noble × 1 copy. Draw 10, first 3 (enhanced: 5) guaranteed from targets.
- **Notes**: Trades Imperious's strong signal on 2 tiles for weaker signal on 5 tiles. Better when you need to find several player tiles at once. Uses same rounded green pip rendering as Imperious.

#### Recall - Sarcastic (alpha: Sarcastic Instructions)
- **Cost**: 2 spoons
- **Exhaust**: No
- **Targeting**: None (immediate)
- **Base effect**: Distribute 10 **red square** (anti-) pips across unrevealed tiles. Uses one of two methods: (1) find a cluster of your tiles and highlight the area around them, or (2) distribute pips weighted toward non-player tiles (neutral ×2, rival ×3, noble ×4 weight, player ×1, with player tiles receiving at most 1 pip).
- **Enhanced effect**: Refunds 1 spoon if another Recall card was already played this floor.
- **Notes**: Anti-pips show where your tiles are NOT (or where a cluster of your tiles is surrounded by). Complementary to Imperious/Vague. The method selection (cluster vs weighted) is determined automatically based on board state. Uses distinct red square pips, different from the rounded green pips used by Imperious/Vague.

### Pip Rendering Note

All Recall cards produce clue pips displayed on tiles. The rendering style distinguishes card types:
- **Imperious / Vague**: Rounded green pips (positive evidence — "this tile might be yours")
- **Sarcastic**: Square red pips (negative evidence — "this tile probably isn't yours")

Pip shape and color are determined by card type, not by the line or position.

## Status Effects

Stage 3 introduces several status effects that need tracking in GameState and display in the UI.

| Status Effect | Source | Behavior | Reset |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Excuses** | Glaze | Stacks. Each stack absorbs one noble reveal (you survive instead of losing). | Per floor |
| **Complaints** | Explode | Stacks. Lose 2 copper per stack at floor end. Reduced by Mollify. | Per floor |
| **Accept Help Discount** | Accept Help | Boolean. All Accept Help cards cost 0 spoons. | Per floor |
| **Distraction** | Ramble | Stacks. Adds noise to rival AI evaluation (no effect until Stage 5). | Per rival turn |

**UI requirements**: Status effects display in the HUD area with an icon and stack count. Hovering over a status effect shows a tooltip describing what it does and how many stacks remain.

### Milestone 17: Card Upgrade System
**Goal**: Cards can be enhanced (stronger effect) or given a bonus spoon (grants 1 spoon after successfully played). Between floors, players choose from: enhance a card, add bonus spoon, or remove a card from their deck.

### Milestone 18: Player Annotation System
**Goal**: Players can manually flag tiles and track owner possibilities. This is the core deduction UI that makes the game deeper.

**Tasks**:
- **Player flagging**: Right-click an unrevealed tile to toggle a "flagged" state (black slash overlay). Flagged = "I don't think this is mine / skip it." This is a visual aid only — no game logic impact.
- **Manual owner exclusion**: Player can click individual quadrants of the owner grid to manually toggle owner possibilities. E.g., click the Noble quadrant to mark "this can't be a noble." Manual exclusions are tracked separately from card-derived annotations.
- **Annotation merging**: Card annotations and player annotations combine (intersection). If a card says {Player, Neutral} and the player manually excludes Neutral, the result is {Player}.
- **Annotation persistence**: Player annotations persist until the tile is revealed or the floor ends. Card annotations also persist.
- **Auto-flagging**: When a tile's owner subset is narrowed to exclude Player (either by cards or player input), auto-flag it (black slash).
- **Saturation detection**: When all tiles of a given type in a row/column/area are accounted for, warn the player before they reveal tiles that are logically determined. (Stretch: this is complex and may slip to Stage 4.)
- **Godot UI**:
  - Right-click toggle for flag
  - Clickable owner quadrants on tiles for manual exclusion
  - Visual distinction between card-derived and player-derived annotations
  - Auto-flag rendering (black slash)

**Data model changes**:
- Add `PlayerAnnotations` to `Tile` or `TileAnnotations`: `HashSet<TileOwner>? PlayerExcluded` (owners the player has manually ruled out)
- Add `bool Flagged` to tile state
- Computed property: `EffectiveOwnerSubset` = intersection of card OwnerSubset and (all owners minus PlayerExcluded)

**Tests** (~10):
- Player can flag/unflag a tile
- Player can exclude an owner type
- Player exclusion intersects with card annotation
- Auto-flag when Player is excluded from owner subset
- Player annotations don't affect game logic (flag is cosmetic)
- Player annotations clear on floor transition
- Multiple exclusions narrow subset correctly

**Status**: Not Started
